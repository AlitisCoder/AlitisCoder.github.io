// about me

Hello, I'm Georgios — student of Applied Artificial Intelligence at Hochschule Heilbronn.

Student of Applied Artificial Intelligence @ Hochschule Heilbronn
I'm learning not just to understand AI, but to actually apply it — from technical fundamentals and machine learning to ethical questions around AI systems. My goal: bridging theory and practice.

Before university, I completed a voluntary social year (FSJ) at ASB, working in their emergency call service (Hausnotruf) — a 24/7 service providing safety to elderly and vulnerable people. In emergencies, I was the first point of contact: assessing situations, coordinating responses, acting quickly and calmly. It taught me more than any course ever could — responsibility, empathy, and a clear head under pressure.

Alongside my studies I keep gaining real-world work experience: as a waiter at Markthalle Ludwigsburg, and previously as a delivery driver for Deutsche Post — reliable, even when things get busy.

In parallel, I build my own coding projects and actively maintain my GitHub with new repositories — because hands-on work matters just as much to me as the theory in the lecture hall.

I'm looking for an internship or working-student position in the AI field where I can contribute and grow.
